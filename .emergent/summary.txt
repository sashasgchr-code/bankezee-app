<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
Build a full-stack web application for a fintech company called Bankezee. The platform should function as a Lead Generation + Sales CRM + Partner Management system.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
- **User Roles:** Admin, Operations Team, Sales Agents, Freelance/Retail Partners, Managers, and Team Leaders.
- **Core Modules:**
    - **User Management:** Role-based sign-up, login, and dashboards. Admin can create, manage, map, and delete all user types.
    - **Hierarchical System:** Admins can create Managers and Team Leaders, map Team Leaders to Managers, and map Agents/Partners to a Manager and an optional Team Leader.
    - **Lead Generation:** Public, Agent, and Partner lead forms.
    - **QR Code System:** Unique QR codes for Agents/Partners to tag leads.
    - **CRM:** Full lead management system with status tracking, lead assignment, and lead deletion. A search bar is available on all dashboards to find leads by name or mobile number.
    - **Dashboards:** Role-specific dashboards with advanced analytics, filtering, and lead lists. Manager/Team Leader dashboards show team performance and their assigned leads.
    - **Commission Tracking:** System to calculate commissions. Earnings stats (Total and Monthly) are displayed on all dashboards.
    - **Document Upload:** System for uploading and associating documents with leads.
    - **Lead Export:** Admin can download lead data and performance stats in CSV format, with filtering options for managers and team leaders.

**User's preferred language**: English
