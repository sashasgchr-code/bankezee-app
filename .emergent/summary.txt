- **User Roles:** Admin, Operations Team, Sales Agents, Freelance/Retail Partners.
- **Core Modules:**

    - **Lead Generation:** Public, Agent, and Partner lead forms.

    - **CRM:** Full lead management system with status tracking, lead assignment, and bulk assignment. Admins can create Ops user accounts.
    - **Eligibility Tracking:** Operations team can track a lead's eligibility with multiple banks, including statuses and rejection reasons.

    - **Commission Tracking:** System to calculate and display agent/partner payouts.
    - **Document Upload:** A system for uploading and associating documents with leads.
    - **Notifications:** SMS/Email alerts for key events (Currently Mocked).
